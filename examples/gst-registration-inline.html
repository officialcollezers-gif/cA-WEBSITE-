01 / REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS

Who needs GST registration?

Requirements depend on turnover, location, nature of supplies, and applicable provisions. These are common situations that may require a closer review.

01

Turnover thresholds

Businesses exceeding applicable limits may need registration. Thresholds and exceptions vary by activity and location.

02

E-commerce activities

Sellers and operators should review the conditions, exemptions, and notifications applicable to their supplies.

03

Specific business situations

Interstate supplies and other specified activities may attract registration requirements, subject to relevant exceptions.

04

Voluntary registration

Eligible businesses can consider voluntary registration after evaluating the benefits and ongoing compliance responsibilities.

04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

How long does registration take?

Timelines depend on document readiness, verification, authority processing, and any queries raised. An indicative timeline can be discussed after reviewing your circumstances.

Will I have ongoing filing obligations?

Registration generally brings ongoing compliance responsibilities. Applicable returns and obligations depend on your registration type and activities.

How are fees and approval handled?

Professional fees depend on the agreed scope and complexity. Registration approval is determined by the relevant authority and cannot be guaranteed.
